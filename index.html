Who We Are

Preamble

We are Filipino Catholics from parishes all across the Diocese of San Diego — families, friends, and neighbors who share a common faith and a common heritage. We come together to pray, celebrate, and serve, and to keep alive the traditions that have shaped who we are.

Whether you were born in the Philippines, grew up here, or are simply curious about our customs, you are welcome. There is always room at the table.

Mission Statement

The Filipino Community of the Roman Catholic Diocese of San Diego exists to nurture the faith of Filipino Catholics and their families, to preserve and share the religious traditions of our heritage, and to work in communion with the Bishop and the parishes of the Diocese for the spiritual growth, dignity, and welfare of all God’s people.

Objectives

  1. Assist in the development of appropriate procedures for implementing diocesan approved programs affecting Filipino Catholics.
  2. Study and prioritize the pastoral needs of the Filipino community in the diocese.
  3. Assist in the establishment of services that are designed to care for the pastoral needs of the Filipino community.
  4. Assist in the maintenance and preservation of the Filipino Catholic traditions and practices.

Events

Four principal observances mark the year of the Filipino Community — Santa Cruzan in May, San Lorenzo Ruiz and San Pedro Calungsod in the autumn, and the nine dawn Masses of Simbang Gabi before the Nativity of the Lord. Each is celebrated in a host parish of the Diocese, and all are welcome, whatever your background or language.

Host parishes, celebrants, and starting times are listed in the diocesan schedule below. Parish coordinators may write to the Commission to volunteer a choir, a procession, or a hospitality committee for any of these celebrations.
